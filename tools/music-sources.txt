WHERE THE FILES COME FROM, AND WHY THEY ARE NOT HERE YET
The licence permits redistribution, but none of these ten tracks has a free file download:
  * vindsvept.se offers free MP3 downloads for three tracks only (Night of the Fae, Onward,
    Enigma), none of them among these ten.
  * Bandcamp is the artist's own download store ("High-quality downloads" in his YouTube
    descriptions): each track page offers MP3, FLAC and more as a PAID digital track,
    USD 1 minimum (USD 1.50 for The Old Way). Bandcamp's own player streams 128 kbps MP3,
    which is a stream and not a download, and was not taken.
So the originals have to be bought (about USD 10.50 for the ten) and passed to
tools/encode_music.sh. Fill the last two columns when that is done.
