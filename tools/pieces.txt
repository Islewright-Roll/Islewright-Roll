Trader (merchant leading a low open hand cart, no canopy): piece_trader.png is 28x19, anchor (bottom centre of the wheels and boots) = (13, 16). Plant the anchor at the hex centre plus (0, 8) so cart and cargo look centred in a 72 px hex.
Trader cargo: draw the 12x12 resource icon with its top-left at sprite pixel (11, -3), that is anchor + (-2, -19). The y is negative on purpose: the icon rises 3 px above the sprite's top row, so do not clip to the sprite bounds. The bottom row of the icon cell lands on the front row of the bed, so icons whose art ends a row or two above the cell bottom (wool, lumber, gold 1; ore, any 2; brick 3) rest on the bed 1 to 3 rows back, which still reads as standing on it. Draw nothing for an empty cart: the bare bed is plain planks. The bed is 17 px wide (sprite x 8 to 24), the icon is centred on it.
Trader spacing: two traders in one hex need at least 30 px between anchors to sit side by side without touching (the sprite is 28 wide). At 16 or 22 px the right merchant stands on the left cart and hides its bed and cargo. If they must be closer, stagger them: offset the anchors by (-9, -5) and (+9, +5), 18 px apart across and 10 px down, which overlaps only a wheel and a boot and keeps both cargoes clear.
Trader footprint: alpha > 128 gives a figure on the left and a long low box with two bumps (wheels) under it on the right, about 28 x 18 px. It reads as a person with a cart if you know to look, and as a plain wide blob if you do not; keep a dark outline or the cargo icon in any hit-test or highlight overlay.
Footprints: every piece has a ground shadow of alpha 96. Build a footprint silhouette from pixels with alpha > 128 (this drops the shadow and keeps one solid blob).
